How it actually works

One 30-minute voice-capture call. We listen. We write. You approve.

  • Once at the start: 30-minute recorded voice-capture call. We ask about what you're building, what frustrates you, where the industry is wrong. The transcript becomes your voice profile.
  • Every week: two LinkedIn posts in draft, written in your voice. You read in five minutes, approve or mark up, ship.
  • Once a month: a 30-minute check-in call to refresh the voice profile against what's actually been happening at your company.
  • Quarterly: we re-tune the voice based on what's landing. The voice gets sharper, not flatter.

The voice match is the entire product. If your post sounds like a McKinsey deck, your friends will assume you outsourced it. Half the work is in the listening, not the writing — which is why most LinkedIn ghostwriting agencies fail.

What we will not write

So you know what you are not buying:

  • Hot takes about politics, religion, or anything where one bad post torches your reputation
  • Anything that puts words in your mouth you would not say to a customer
  • Engagement-bait threads ("3 things every founder must do!"), unless you genuinely love them
  • Cross-platform — LinkedIn only. Twitter, Substack, podcasts: hire a different shop

How we know it is working

  1. A friend who sees your posts says "did you write this?" and you say yes — because it sounds like you.
  2. You stop dreading the LinkedIn tab.

Followers and impressions are downstream of those three things. We track them, but they are not the goal.
