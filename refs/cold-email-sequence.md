**Target:** VP Facilities, Director of Engineering, Director of Sustainability, Plant Superintendent, Energy Manager, CFO at NorCal C&I sites with an active RFP / RFQ for distributed generation, cogen replacement, or BTM resilience.
**Send domain:** `info@bcalenergy.com` (existing infrastructure).
**Sender name in chrome:** Bharath
**Cadence:** 3-touch over 14 days. Pause on reply.
**Single-product rule (G7):** every email mentions only the RFP Evaluation offer. Never mentions Pivot B, never mentions Bcal's developer business beyond the recusal note.

## ⚡ MANDATORY ATTACHMENT — every Day-0 cold (v2 rule, locked 2026-05-06)

Every Day-0 cold send MUST attach a per-account qualification one-pager generated by the Bcal Site Qualification Platform (`qualify.bcalenergy.com`) for the recipient's specific site address. The body of the email references the attached PDF by name (e.g., "I've attached the Bcal qualification one-pager for {{site_indicator}} — tariff-impact band, SGIP queue verdict, IRA bonus stack, hosting capacity, and permit timeline, all source-cited.").

**G4 OVERRIDE (BCAL only):** the standing G4 "no attachments on cold sends" rule is overridden for BCAL outbound by CEO directive 2026-05-06. The override is logged in `nightly_audit.md` with deliverability impact to be measured at the 30-day mark and revisited.

**File-naming convention:** `Qualify_Onepager_<AccountShortName>_<YYYY-MM-DD>.pdf` — example `Qualify_Onepager_FSSD_2026-05-06.pdf`. Pre-generated PDFs live in `Bcal Energy/qualification_platform/output/` and `Bcal Energy/acquisitions/`.

**Generation source:** `qualify.bcalenergy.com` for new addresses, OR pre-generated PDF for any account already in the qualified list. Schema: `qualification_platform/schemas/qualification_report.json`. PDF design: `qualification_platform/v3_customer_report/v3_1_onepage/`.

**Do not send if no qualification PDF.** A cold without the per-account PDF reverts to the v1 sequence body and queues for next-batch send only after the qualification run completes. The send pipeline must check for `qualify_pdf_path` on every row and hold the row if missing.

**Day-7 follow-up and Day-14 break-up do NOT re-attach the PDF.** They reference it by name ("the qualification one-pager I sent on {{day_0_date}}") and rely on continued thread context.

**Required tokens per send (G6, ≥3 distinct):** first_name, organization, RFP_identifier (number or short title), bid_open_date OR bid_close_date, technology_scope (e.g., "1.25 MW cogen replacement", "BTM resilience"), funding_program if public (SGIP, SRA, ECAA).

If fewer than 3 distinct tokens are verified for a contact, do not send.
