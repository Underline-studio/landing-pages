## What's in scope

For each vendor response in your stack, Bcal produces:

- **Tariff-impact line** — modeled net bill delta vs. your current PG&E (or SCE/SMUD) schedule using the actual meter's tariff and the vendor's stated runtime profile. Cited to the tariff sheet revision and date.
- **SGIP eligibility verdict** — funding window status (current step, remaining budget at the IOU level), category fit (Standard / Equity / Equity Resiliency), biogas adder applicability. Cited to the latest SGIP semi-annual budget report.
- **IRA bonus stack** — base ITC, energy-community bonus, low-income census-tract bonus, domestic-content posture as claimed by the vendor. Cited to the IRS guidance document and a census-tract lookup.
- **Hosting-capacity verdict** — PG&E ICA reading at the meter (or equivalent for SCE/SMUD). Cited to the public hosting-capacity map snapshot date.
- **AHJ permit risk** — building, electrical, mechanical, fire pathway with named planner or counter contact where public, plus zoning verdict. Cited to municipal code section.
- **Air-permit verdict** (combustion options only) — BAAQMD / SJVAPCD / SCAQMD permit pathway, BACT exposure, offset requirement. Cited to the district's most recent rule revision.
- **Vendor-claim audit** — every quantitative claim in the vendor response (efficiency, capacity factor, capex, O&M) checked against the OEM's public spec sheet and the FERC Form 1 / EIA 923 record where applicable. Discrepancies flagged.

Output: a 12–18 page PDF memo with a 1-page executive summary, a ranked scoring table, and an appendix containing the source-cited per-vendor analysis.
