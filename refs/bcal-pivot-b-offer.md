## What this is

You have a single site under consideration for distributed generation, behind-the-meter resilience, or cogen replacement. You've already gotten contradictory answers — PG&E's hosting-capacity tool says one thing, an EPC's verbal estimate says another, the local AHJ says a third. You don't yet have an RFP out. You need a written, source-cited answer to one question:

**Can this project be permitted and interconnected at this site, on what timeline, and with what funding window?**

Bcal delivers a 7-day written feasibility PDF. 8–12 pages. Source-cited at every numeric claim. CFO and facilities can both sign it.

## What's in scope

For your single site, Bcal produces a written feasibility covering:
- **PG&E hosting capacity at the meter** — ICA reading, snapshot date, supportable export and BTM postures.
- **SGIP funding window status** — current step at PG&E (or SCE/SoCalGas), remaining IOU budget, category fit.
- **IRA bonus eligibility** — base ITC, energy-community bonus, low-income census-tract bonus, domestic-content posture.
- **AHJ permit pathway** — building, electrical, mechanical, fire, zoning. Named planner contact where public.
- **Air-permit verdict** (combustion options) — BAAQMD / SJVAPCD / SCAQMD pathway, BACT exposure, offsets.
- **Tariff-impact band** — modeled net bill delta against your current schedule.
- **One-paragraph go / wait / walk recommendation.**

Output: 8–12 page PDF with executive summary, analysis, source register, hosting-capacity screenshot.
